This is the first real issue of the LiquidSea digest — thinner than a typical week, but every figure in it is checked against multiple independent sources rather than lifted from a single press release.

Indonesia carried the week. FLOQ, founded by former Tokocrypto CEO Yudhono Rawis, closed its first outside funding at $11.3M from a cluster of private and strategic investors. It's modest next to the region's headline rounds, but notable as capital betting on digital-asset infrastructure — custody, compliance, stablecoin rails — rather than pure trading volume.

Future issues will widen out as more disclosed rounds across Singapore, Thailand, Vietnam, the Philippines and Malaysia clear our verification bar. See [CONTRIBUTING-DATA.md](https://github.com/workarounds81/liquidsea/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING-DATA.md) for exactly what that bar is.
